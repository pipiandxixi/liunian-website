Support

Liunian is made by one person. Write to support@liunianai.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Screenshots help. We reply as soon as we can.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Liunian has no sign-in. Your notes live on your iPhone and sync through your own iCloud when iCloud is on.

Does Liunian work offline?

Yes. Recording voice, photo, video and text notes—and default on-device speech transcription—work on your iPhone without an internet connection. iCloud sync and Plus AI features require a network connection.

How is Liunian different from Apple Voice Memos or a traditional diary app?

Liunian brings hold-to-talk voice notes, short videos (up to 30 seconds on Free, 90 seconds on Plus), photos and text onto one timeline. Voice notes and videos are transcribed on your iPhone by default so you can search what you said, see notes on a map with Footprint, turn notes into To-Dos or reminders, or connect people and topics in a personal knowledge base.

How do To-Do, reminders and Add to Calendar work?

You decide which notes need follow-through: add any note to the built-in To-Do list, save it in Review Later, or give it a reminder. When a note has a reminder, choose Add to Calendar to review and confirm a separate event in the iPhone's calendar editor. Later edits in Liunian do not update that calendar copy.

Can I export my notes to Obsidian or Markdown?

Yes. With Plus, the personal knowledge base organizes the people, places and topics mentioned in your notes and lets you export them as Markdown files for continued use in tools such as Obsidian.

How do I manage or cancel Plus?

Plus is an auto-renewing subscription billed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also manage it from the plan page inside Liunian. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Plus until the period you paid for ends.

How do I restore Plus on a new iPhone?

Open the plan page in Liunian and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

My notes aren't syncing between devices.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud Drive is on, and that Liunian is allowed to use iCloud in Settings › your name › iCloud. Sync can take a minute or two on a good connection.

What does Liunian send when I use AI?

Only what an AI feature needs for that request, and only after you agree the first time. Voice notes are transcribed on your iPhone by default. The privacy policy lists exactly what each feature sends.

How do I delete my data?

To remove your notes and their files from your device and iCloud, open Liunian Settings › Data › Delete all data. You can also delete individual notes. Our server does not keep your notes, audio or photos; see the privacy policy for the limited AI results and service records it keeps. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to support@liunianai.com.

Which languages does Liunian support?

English and Simplified Chinese. If your iPhone uses another language, Liunian shows English.
