Voice, photo and video notes for iPhone

A notebook for the moments you'd otherwise forget.

Hold to talk, snap a photo, or type a line. Liunian keeps it on your timeline, in your own iCloud, and writes your day back to you.

Features

Say it. Shoot it. Find it again.

Voice and camera first: the two fastest ways to keep a moment. Everything else is there to help you find it later.

Hold to talk

Press and speak. Let go and it's on your timeline, already transcribed on your iPhone.

Hold to shoot video

Tap for a photo, hold for a video. Up to 30 seconds, kept with its time and place.

From video to words

What was said in a recording or video is written out, searchable, and yours to edit.

Footprint

Your notes on a map, so a place brings its memories back.

Knowledge base Plus

The people, places and topics in your notes, and how they connect. Open any one to see every note that mentions it, or export it all as Markdown for Obsidian.

Dashboard

A month of your notes at a glance: what you captured, when, and what you still meant to do.

And more

The small things that make it yours.

A notebook should get out of the way, and still be there when you need it.

  • Timeline and search. Grouped by day, searchable across your words and transcripts.
  • To-Do, Reminders and Review Later. Turn any note into something to do, once or on repeat.
  • Five note styles. Default, Sticker, Journal, Washi and Manga, in light and dark.
  • iCloud sync. Your notes sync through your own iCloud. No account to create.
