Liunian

A notebook for the moments you'd otherwise forget. Hold to talk, snap a photo, or type a line.

Coming soon to the App Store

Capture in a second

Hold to talk

Voice notes are transcribed on your iPhone. Photos and videos get their words transcribed too.

Time and place

Every note keeps when it was written and, if you allow it, where.

Find your way back

A timeline by day, search across words and transcripts, and Footprint, your notes on a map.

Ask your notes

Ask a question the way you'd ask a friend, "When did I last go to Kyoto?", and Liunian answers from your own notes, pointing to the ones it used.

Plus

Ask without a monthly limit, AI Daily (a written recap of any day), summaries of voice notes and videos, a knowledge base that maps the people, places and topics in your notes, and recordings three times as long. Plus is an auto-renewing monthly subscription.

Yours, and private

There is no account to create. Your notes stay on your device and sync through your own iCloud, which we cannot read. Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you choose to use an AI feature, and Liunian tells you what it sends before it does. We don't sell your data, show ads, or train AI models on your notes. Read the privacy policy.

Make it yours

Five note styles, Default, Sticker, Journal, Washi and Manga, in light and dark.
