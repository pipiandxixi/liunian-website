Ways to use Liunian

Some things happen once.
Some ideas arrive unannounced.

Keep the view from a ferry, a sentence you don't want to forget, or an idea that interrupts your coffee. A few seconds or one line is enough; you can decide what to do with it later.

Moment capturing

Keep a little more than a photo.

A scene is more than what the camera sees. A short clip and the words you said can be the whole entry; no full diary page required.

01 / On the ferry

The water changes color. You have ten seconds.

Open Liunian's camera and hold for a short video. The crossing lands on your timeline with its time and, if you've allowed location, its place. You can keep this little moment without stopping to title or organize it.

  1. Tap the camera
  2. Hold to record
  3. Let go to keep it

Free video notes can be up to 30 seconds; Plus allows up to 90 seconds.

02 / After the rain

Tell the clip what your eyes noticed.

Film the reflections and say, “The whole hill looks like it's underwater.” Liunian keeps the video and writes out your words on your iPhone. That sentence preserves what caught your attention; later, search it instead of scrolling through every clip.

  1. Record a short video
  2. Speak while filming
  3. Open its transcript later
When a place brings it back

That corner again?

Open Footprint to see notes on a map and revisit what you captured there. Location is optional; your notes remain private.

Idea capturing

Catch the thought before it turns into “what was it?”

Ideas rarely wait for a blank page. Save the rough version first, by voice, text, or sharing from another app. It can stay a thought until you choose to revisit it.

03 / Between one thing and the next

Say it while it still makes sense.

You're walking out the door when a thought shows up. Hold the voice button, speak naturally, and let go. The recording and its transcript become a note you can search later, rather than another clip to replay from the start. You can add Liunian's button to your Lock Screen or Control Center to open it ready to record after unlocking.

  1. Hold to talk
  2. Release to save
  3. Find the words later
04 / At the coffee shop

“Let people make their first note in ten seconds.”

At a coffee shop, on a call, or in a quiet office, saying an idea out loud may not work. Type the one sentence that clicked. Keep a photo if it helps you remember the setting. If the thought needs action later, add a reminder or put the note on your To-Do list yourself; it doesn't need a deadline just to be saved.

  1. Write the idea
  2. Add a photo if you like
  3. Set a reminder when needed
05 / While reading something else

Save the spark, not just the link.

Find a line in Safari that gives you an idea? Share the link, text or photo to Liunian, and add a sentence about why it mattered. It lands in Review Later, where you can return to it on your own terms instead of leaving another unexplained bookmark behind.
