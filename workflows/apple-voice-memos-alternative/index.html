Beyond Audio Lists · Free

Voice, short video, photo, and text on one timeline.

Stop hunting across Voice Memos, Photos, and Notes to piece together one day. Keep every kind of capture side by side, automatically transcribed and grouped by day.

01 / Unified capture & search

Hold to talk. Hold to shoot video. Search what you said.

In Liunian, a single capture bar lets you press and hold for a voice note, tap for a photo, hold for a short video (up to 30 seconds on Free, 90 seconds on Plus), or type a quick line. Both voice notes and spoken words inside videos are transcribed on your iPhone with timestamps—so you can search across written notes and spoken transcripts instantly.

  1. Voice, video, photo, or text
  2. Automatic on-device transcript
  3. Search every word on the timeline

Jump across months using the timeline rail, and customize your timeline with five visual styles (Default, Sticker, Journal, Washi, Manga) in light or dark mode.

Intentional Follow-Through · Free

From a quick recording to To-Do, Review Later, and iPhone Calendar.

Not every passing thought belongs on your task list—and an agreed meeting time belongs on your calendar. You stay in control of what becomes an action item.

02 / To-Do, Review Later & Calendar

Keep the full audio and photo context behind every task.

Manually pin any note to your built-in To-Do list or save links shared from Safari into Review Later so rough ideas don't clutter active tasks. Set a one-time or repeating reminder on any note, or tap Add to Calendar to inspect and confirm a separate event in the iPhone's native calendar editor.

  1. Choose notes that need action
  2. Add to To-Do or set a reminder
  3. Confirm in iPhone Calendar

Liunian never auto-creates tasks from every spoken sentence, and Add to Calendar creates a separate confirmed event rather than a two-way calendar sync.

Spatial Memory & Monthly Review

Revisit notes by place with Footprint and monthly Dashboard.

Remembering where you were often brings back what you thought of. Explore your notes spatially and track your month at a glance.

03 / Footprint Map & Plus AI

Your private map of moments—plus optional AI organization.

When optional location permission is granted, Footprint places your notes on a private map so revisiting a neighborhood or trip brings back what you recorded there. Check the monthly Dashboard to see capture habits and open To-Dos. Upgrade to Plus anytime (14-day free trial) for 3× longer recordings, AI Daily recaps, Polished Transcript, and the Personal Knowledge Base with Markdown export for Obsidian.

  1. Browse notes on Footprint map
  2. Review monthly Dashboard
  3. Optional Plus for AI & Obsidian
