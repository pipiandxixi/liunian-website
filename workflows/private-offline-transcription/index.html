Privacy & On-Device Transcription

Voice and video notes that stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

A personal notebook should not require creating another cloud account or sending every spoken thought to a third-party server. Liunian runs speech transcription on your iPhone by default and syncs strictly through your own Apple iCloud.

Works offline · No sign-up or profile · One-step data deletion

Default Local Processing · Free

On-device speech transcription by default.

Most AI note apps upload your audio to external servers just to turn speech into text. Liunian transcribes directly on your iPhone—free, unlimited, and even without an internet connection.

01 / Offline-ready capture

Speak on a plane, a trail, or anywhere offline.

Every voice note (up to 60 seconds on Free, 3 minutes on Plus) and short video (up to 30 seconds on Free, 90 seconds on Plus) is transcribed locally on your iPhone with timestamps. Nothing leaves your device to generate transcripts, so your timeline stays private and searchable across both written text and spoken words.

  1. Hold to talk or shoot video
  2. Transcribed on your iPhone
  3. Search offline any time

The Lock Screen and Control Center button opens Liunian straight to the recording screen after you unlock your iPhone—never covertly while locked.

Zero Account Architecture · Free

No Liunian account. Synced through your private iCloud.

You shouldn't have to hand over an email address or create a user profile to keep a daily journal or voice notebook.

02 / Private CloudKit sync

Your notes live in your storage, not ours.

Liunian has no sign-up, login, user profile, or ad tracking. Notes, photos, voice recordings, videos, reminders, and To-Dos stay on your iPhone and, when iCloud is enabled, sync exclusively through your own Apple iCloud private database—which we cannot access or read.

  1. Open Liunian—no login wall
  2. Store locally on iPhone
  3. Sync via your own iCloud

Need a fresh start? Delete individual notes or clear all notes, media, AI Daily summaries, and your knowledge base from device and iCloud in one step via Settings → Data → Delete all data.

Explicit User Control Plus

Transparent consent when you choose Plus AI features.

By default, zero data leaves your device and private iCloud. Cloud AI only runs when you explicitly choose an optional feature—and never without telling you first.

03 / Opt-in AI & strict boundaries

Only what is needed, only after you agree. Plus

When you choose Plus AI features—AI Daily, Polished Transcript, or the Personal Knowledge Base (with Markdown export for Obsidian)—or optional cloud transcription, Liunian explains exactly what content will be sent and asks for your consent first. Only the specific text or request required is sent.
