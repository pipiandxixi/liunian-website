Obsidian & PKM Workflow

From a spoken thought on iPhone to clean Markdown in Obsidian.

Capture fleeting ideas hands-on by voice without breaking your stride (Free), then turn spoken transcripts into polished prose, a connected knowledge graph, and Markdown files ready for your Obsidian vault with Plus.

1. Capture on iPhone ↓2. Polish & Recap ↓3. Export to Obsidian ↓

No account required · On-device transcription (Free) · Markdown export (Plus)

Step 01 · Free Core

Capture without breaking your stride.

Typing on a small screen interrupts the flow of an idea. Speak naturally on iPhone and let Liunian handle the raw capture and transcription locally—free, unlimited, and with zero account setup.

01 / Fast capture & Share Sheet

Hold to talk. Release to transcribe on device.

Press and hold the microphone bar in Liunian to record a voice note (up to 60 seconds on Free, 3 minutes on Plus). Add Liunian's button to your Lock Screen or Control Center to jump straight to the recording screen after unlocking your iPhone. Every voice note and short video is transcribed directly on your iPhone with timestamps and synced through your private iCloud.

  1. Open via Lock Screen or tap
  2. Hold to talk
  3. Searchable transcript saved

Reading in Safari or another app? Use the Share Sheet extension to save links, text, or photos to Review Later with a quick note on why they mattered.

Step 02 · Structured Text Plus

Turn rambling speech into clean written text.

Spoken brainstorms are full of pauses, false starts, and filler words. Liunian Plus helps refine raw voice transcripts into readable notes you will actually want to keep in your vault.

02 / Polished Transcript & AI Daily

Clean prose from spoken notes, without losing your voice. Plus

Run Polished Transcript on any voice or video note to turn raw speech into clean, structured written text free of filler words and repetition. At the end of a busy day, run AI Daily to synthesize that day's scattered voice, photo, video, and text entries into a cohesive daily recap.

  1. Review raw timestamped transcript
  2. Generate Polished Transcript
  3. Or run AI Daily for a day's recap

AI features are part of the optional Plus subscription and send only the specific note content needed for your request after explaining what is sent and getting your consent.

Step 03 · Knowledge Graph & Export Plus

Connect people, places & topics — then export Markdown for Obsidian.

Ideas accumulate across weeks. Instead of letting voice memos pile up in folders, organize mentions into a connected graph and bring them into your permanent Obsidian system.

03 / Personal Knowledge Base

Entity pages with provenance, exported as Markdown. Plus

Enable the Personal Knowledge Base in Liunian Plus to connect recurring people, places, projects, and topics mentioned across your timeline. Open any entity to see linked notes and jump back to the original source audio, video, or text. When you're ready to archive or expand in Obsidian, export your knowledge base as clean Markdown files.

  1. Enable Knowledge Base (Plus)
  2. Browse connected entities
  3. Export Markdown for Obsidian

Plus includes a 14-day free trial for new subscribers and can be managed or cancelled any time in your Apple Account settings.

Clear Free vs. Plus boundaries

What's Free vs. what requires Plus

Capture and transcribe every day for free—upgrade to Plus when you want AI organization and Obsidian Markdown export.

Free ($0)

  • Unlimited voice & video capture: voice up to 60s, video up to 30s each.
  • Unlimited on-device transcripts with timestamps and full-text timeline search.
  • To-Do, Reminders, Review Later, Add to Calendar, Footprint map, Dashboard, Lock Screen button, Share Sheet, and private iCloud sync.
  • Zero account required and zero third-party data processing by default.

Plus 14-day free trial

Plus is an auto-renewing monthly subscription billed through your Apple Account. Explore Private On-Device Transcription or Apple Voice Memos Alternative.
